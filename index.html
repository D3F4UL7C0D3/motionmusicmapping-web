01 — Instrument

The Theremin: music played in the air.

A camera follows both hands. Their height, distance and shape become pitch, volume and effects, with nothing to touch. Anyone can play it within seconds; playing it well takes practice, like any instrument.

Video coming soon
  • Its own voiceA built-in sound engine, so it plays on its own: no studio or computer music software needed.
  • A controller for Ableton LiveOn stage it can also drive Ableton Live over MIDI, with a table of effects that each hand can reach.
  • Made for the publicBuilt to be picked up by passers-by: walk up, raise your hands, and you are already playing.
02 — Performance

Motion: projection mapping you can play.

Our own software for projecting onto real objects: boxes, walls and facades. The picture is cut and bent onto every surface, and it listens to the music band by band, from the kick to the hi-hats.

Video coming soon
  • Played live with a Novation Launchkey Mini MK3Every knob, pad and strip has a job: pick visuals, bend them, launch scenes, black out.
  • Forty visuals that hearEach control can follow its own range of frequencies, so the image moves with the song, not just with the volume.
  • Hands as controllersThe same hand tracking as the Theremin can move the picture, so a performer or the audience can play the light.
03 — Formats

Two ways to meet it.

Interactive installation

For festivals and public space. Visitors play the light and the sound themselves, alone or together. Scales from one projector to several.

Live audiovisual performance

For clubs, stages and audiovisual events. Theremin, Ableton Live and Motion played together: the music and the mapping in one set.
